Hue Bridge

Needed once on newer bridges: accept the security warning in the tab that opens, then come back.
Tap Pair, then press the round link button on the bridge within 30 seconds. Or paste a key you created another way (see the README).

Kiosk

Locking hides Cues, Keypad and Setup and all editing, so the tablet is safe to leave out for the household. Unlock with the padlock and your PIN.

Show

Put a show exported from the desktop HueDesk next to this file on your web server, then load it here by name.

Help

  • Play: executors with GO, BACK, OFF and FLASH. Drag a fader up and down; tapping it does nothing, so it can't be knocked by accident.
  • Lights: tap tiles or chips to select, then pick a colour and brightness. STORE saves the look to an executor or as a scene.
  • Long-press an executor, effect, colour, scene or macro to edit it (when unlocked).
  • Keypad accepts the same commands as the desktop, e.g. Group 2 At 50, Go Exec 1.

Running as a kiosk

  1. Serve this file on your home network, e.g. dotnet serve -p 8080 or python -m http.server 8080 on an always-on PC or Raspberry Pi.
  2. On the tablet open http://<server-ip>:8080/HueDesk_Tablet.html, then in Setup tap Trust certificate and accept the bridge's security warning.
  3. iPad: Share > Add to Home Screen, open it from the icon, then turn on Guided Access (Settings > Accessibility) and triple-click to lock the iPad to HueDesk.
  4. Android: Chrome > Add to Home screen, or use a kiosk browser such as Fully Kiosk Browser.
  5. Lock HueDesk with a PIN and choose the idle options above.
